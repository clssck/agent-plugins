# Source, Intent & Criticality Collection

## Summary
Collect the source data reference, transformation intent, and pipeline criticality. Last interactive task before silent investigation begins.

## Personas
- Data Engineer
- Data Architect

## Details
## **What You Will Accomplish**

- Provide a reference to your source data (table, schema, database, or stage)
- Describe what you want the transformation to produce
- Declare how critical this pipeline is (drives testing and monitoring depth)

## **Steps in This Task**

| Step | Title | Purpose | Conditional |
|------|-------|---------|-------------|
| 4.1 | Source Seed | Collect source data reference | Always |
| 4.2 | Define Intent | What should the pipeline produce? | Always |
| 4.3 | Pipeline Criticality | Determine testing/monitoring depth | Always |

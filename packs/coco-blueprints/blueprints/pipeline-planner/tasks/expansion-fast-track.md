# Expansion Fast-Track

## Summary
Collect technology, freshness, and consumer answers for expansion users who are continuing with their existing technology.

## Personas
- Data Engineer

## Details
## **What You Will Accomplish**

- Identify which technology your existing pipeline uses
- Define freshness requirements for the new expansion
- Identify downstream consumers of the expanded output

## **Steps in This Task**

| Step | Title | Purpose | Conditional |
|------|-------|---------|-------------|
| 2.1 | Expansion Technology Selection | Which tech are you using? | Same Technology only |
| 2.2 | Expansion Freshness | How current must the output be? | Same Technology only |
| 2.3 | Expansion Consumers | What consumes the output? | Same Technology only |
